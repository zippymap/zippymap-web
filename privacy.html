Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the data practices of ZippyMap ("App"), operated by The Developer.

1. Information Collected

Google Sign-In: We collect Full Name and Email Address for account management.

Service Providers: We collect phone numbers to allow users to contact you directly.

Location: Precise GPS coordinates are processed only for Service Providers when "Live Location" is enabled.

2. Purpose

Data is used for identity verification and core map functionality (finding nearby help).

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Play Services, Firebase Authentication, and Firestore for secure data storage.
