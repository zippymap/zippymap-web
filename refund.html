Refund and Cancellation Policy

1. Cancellations

As ZippyMap is a platform for real-time discovery, users may stop using the service at any time by disabling their live location or logging out. Service provider registrations can be cancelled by contacting zippymap.team@gmail.com.

2. Refunds

Currently, ZippyMap provides platform access. In the event of any future paid services or accidental duplicate payments, refunds will be processed to the original payment source within 10-15 business days. For any payment-related disputes, please contact our support email.
